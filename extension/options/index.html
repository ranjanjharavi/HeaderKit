Review how ReqKit handles your data

ReqKit reads tab URLs locally for the current host and toolbar badges, including in the background on sites you grant access to. URLs are not stored or sent to the developer.

Profiles, settings, and header values are stored unencrypted in this Chrome profile. Anyone with access to its files may be able to read them.

ReqKit has no analytics or developer server. Active header values go only to their exact HTTPS host with site access; destination or intermediary logs may include them.
